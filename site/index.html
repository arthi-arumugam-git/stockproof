stockproof

reorder, purchase orders and labels for Shopify nothing leaves this tab
Shopify retired Stocky on 31 August 2026. This page does the three things merchants said they missed most: suggest what to reorder, print a real purchase order, and print barcode labels. It runs entirely in your browser — your exports are read in this tab and never sent anywhere.

1 · Load your exports

Inventory required

Products → Inventory → Export. Choose All states.

Orders for suggestions

Orders → Export. Pick a recent window, 30 to 90 days works well.

Products for cost and vendor

Products → Export. Adds supplier, cost and barcode to each line.

40 products, two locations, 60 days of orders. Made up, so you can see the whole thing work before exporting anything real.

Drag files anywhere on this page and each one is recognised by its columns, so the order does not matter.
